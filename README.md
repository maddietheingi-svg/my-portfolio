# Myint Myat Theingi - Portfolio Website

Your professional portfolio is ready to deploy! Follow these simple steps:

---

## 🚀 Quick Deploy (Recommended)

### Option A: Deploy with Vercel (Easiest - 5 minutes)

1. **Create accounts** (if you don't have them):
   - GitHub: https://github.com/signup
   - Vercel: https://vercel.com/signup (use "Continue with GitHub")

2. **Upload to GitHub**:
   - Go to https://github.com/new
   - Name your repository `my-portfolio`
   - Keep it **Public**
   - Click **Create repository**
   - Follow the instructions to upload these files (or drag & drop the folder)

3. **Deploy on Vercel**:
   - Go to https://vercel.com/new
   - Click **Import** next to your `my-portfolio` repository
   - Click **Deploy**
   - Wait ~1 minute
   - 🎉 Your site is live at `https://my-portfolio-xxxxx.vercel.app`

### Option B: Deploy with Netlify

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag and drop this entire folder
3. Done! Your site is live.

## 🔗 Custom Domain (Optional)

After deploying, you can add a custom domain:

**On Vercel:**
1. Go to your project dashboard
2. Click **Settings** → **Domains**
3. Add your domain (e.g., `maddietheingi.com`)
4. Follow DNS instructions

**On Netlify:**
1. Go to **Site settings** → **Domain management**
2. Click **Add custom domain**
3. Follow the setup wizard
